# Phase Two: virtual try-on renderer decision (2026-11-25)
## Implemented
Local SVG illustration of two original garments and an open-jacket layering demonstration. Landmark-based curved paths, seam and fold accents, gradient shading and clipped texture patterns. No photographic inputs, network inference, physical drape prediction or retailer product claims.
## Photographic renderer candidates
- FASHN VTON v1.5: https://github.com/fashn-AI/fashn-vton-1.5 and https://huggingface.co/fashn-ai/fashn-vton-1.5. Upstream model and main code Apache-2.0; documented person + garment inputs, tops/bottoms/one-pieces. Upstream notes ~2 GB main/pose weights and additional ~244 MB parser weights; dedicated GPU recommended. The bundled human parser inherits the NVIDIA SegFormer license (https://huggingface.co/fashn-ai/fashn-human-parser). Do not assume entire pipeline is commercially cleared. Review all dependencies, model weights, input/output rights, and potentially replace or license parser before production.
- IDM-VTON: https://huggingface.co/yisol/IDM-VTON and https://github.com/yisol/IDM-VTON. Noncommercial checkpoint terms (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); not selected for commercial deployment.
## Decision
No model or external endpoint enabled. No new compute charges. First evaluate a commercially cleared pipeline against original or properly licensed synthetic person and garment fixtures, isolated from production. Measure preservation of identity, garment detail, silhouette, occlusion, failure rates, latency, resource usage and cost. Verify model/data licenses and privacy, explicit consent, storage isolation and deletion before enabling customer photos. Generated appearance is not fit or sizing evidence. Realistic rendering remains open until a model is evaluated and integrated; the local illustration is a fallback and test harness, not the final product.
