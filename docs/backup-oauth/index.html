Shadow Development Backups

Shadow Development Backups is an internal backup utility used by Shadow Development LLC to securely store and verify encrypted copies of authorized application data in a Google Drive account controlled by the organization.

What it does

Uses rclone and Google Drive to upload encrypted backup archives, confirm successful transfers, and retrieve archives for recovery testing when needed.

Google Drive access

Requests the limited Google Drive file-access scope (drive.file), used for files and folders created or opened by this backup utility—not unrestricted access to every Drive file.

Who uses it

This is an administrator-operated utility for Shadow Development LLC, not a public consumer application. It is separate from the shopper and retailer sign-in experience.

Protecting backups

Backup archives are encrypted before they are uploaded. The backup destination is not a public file-sharing service, and encryption private keys are kept separate from the stored archives.

This website is informational only. It does not ask visitors to sign in with Google or collect Google Drive data.

For details about authorized Google Drive access, retention and deletion, please read our Privacy Policy.
