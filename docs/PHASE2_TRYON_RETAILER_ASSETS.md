# Retailer garment intake — Phase Two
The shared catalog already supplies product_master, product_variant and retailer_offer records via the shopper catalog adapter. The new garment catalog module uses explicit category mappings for tops, bottoms and one-piece garments; suits, footwear, intimate apparel and unknown categories are not enabled. A shopper can select a real catalog color/size reference, not a fabricated option. This is independent of the recommended size, stock, price and wishlist.
## Offline intake
Use reviewGarmentAsset in scripts/tryon/reviewGarmentAsset.mjs with a trusted catalog export and retailer-provided asset bytes. Exact active product, color variant, active variant-specific retailer offer and business UUID must match. Offers without an explicit variant or retailer business are ineligible. Record explicit commercial derivative/virtual try-on rights, reviewer, supporting evidence, validity interval and revocation status. Record a human image review for clean background, front view, one garment, color accuracy and absence of a person. Compare SHA-256 to the actual bytes and validate JPEG/PNG headers and declared dimensions.
Passing is an offline intake screening result only. Header checks are not full image decoding, metadata does not authenticate the reviewer or prove rights, and no provider may consume the result directly. Before real ingestion, implement trusted reviewer authorization, immutable rights and revocation records, complete decode/metadata stripping, asset storage isolation, exact catalog linkage and revalidation, provenance tracking and qualified renderer integration. Do not copy or infer license from a public product listing. No raw image URLs are returned or fetched by the new shopper component.
